# ECOMMERCE-RETAIL-DASHBOARD-USING-TABLEAU
Interactive E-Commerce sales analytics dashboard built in Tableau , analyzing 400K+ transactions across customers, products, countries, and sales trends.

# Tools Used
Tableau (calculated fields, dashboard actions, Top-N filtering).

# What it does:
Built calculated fields to clean invalid data (cancelled orders, missing customer IDs, invalid prices, unspecified countries).

Created KPI summary, monthly sales trend, country-wise and weekday sales breakdown.

Ranked views of Top 10 products and Top 10 customers by revenue.

# Key Insights:
Top product: Stock code 22423 is the best-seller, with a significant ~42K unit gap over the second-best product (8523A) - indicating strong demand concentration in a single SKU.

Top customer: Customer 14646 is the highest-value customer by revenue; the top 10 customers drive a disproportionate share of total sales, suggesting a customer retention strategy should prioritize this segment.

Monthly trend: September and November are the strongest sales months; overall trend is positive with fluctuation.

Weekly pattern: Thursdays and Tuesdays generate the most sales, weekends the least-suggesting marketing campaigns could be timed around high-performing weekdays for maximum impact.

Geography: UK dominates at -84% of total sales, with Netherlands a distant second at -3.3%, highlighting heavy market concentration in a single country.
